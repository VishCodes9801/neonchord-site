Neonchord

From an idea to a finished song.

Neonchord is a music studio for iPhone that writes with you. Pick a vibe, play along on chord pads that always sound right, and turn your loop into a full song with one tap.

Start with an ideaEight styles, from Lo-Fi to Trap to House. A beat, bass, chords and a melody start playing in a fitting key.
Play alongOne-finger chord pads, an in-key keyboard and drum pads. Every note fits the song.
Build the songIntros, verses, choruses, fills, risers and drops are arranged for you.
Write and recordLyrics sit right next to the music, so you can read along while you record your voice.
Finish and shareMix, master and share your song as audio or a vertical video, or back it up as a song file.

Free, with Neonchord Pro for more

Making songs is free: four vibes, Short and Classic arrangements, every instrument except the Sampler, recording, lyrics, editing, AAC export and videos (with a small “Made with Neonchord” line).

Neonchord Pro adds every vibe (R&B, Synthwave, Afrobeats, Ambient), Full Song and Club arrangements, Tune, the Sampler, Mix It For Me, WAV export, streaming mastering, stems, MIDI export and clean videos.

Pro is available as a monthly or yearly subscription (the yearly plan may include a free trial) or as a one-time Lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app in your currency before you buy. Support

Where are my songs saved?

On your iPhone, inside Neonchord. They're saved automatically while you work and are included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup.

How do I back up a song or move it to another iPhone?

Open the song, tap ••• → Share Song File (or use ••• next to the song on the home screen). This makes one .neonchord file with the song and its recordings. Save it to Files or iCloud Drive, or AirDrop it. On the other iPhone, tap the file or use Import on the home screen.

How do I restore Pro on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then open Settings in Neonchord (the gear on the home screen) and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In Neonchord, open Settings → Manage Subscription, or on your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Neonchord. Pro stays unlocked until the end of the period you've paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Recording doesn't start.

Neonchord needs microphone access to record. Turn it on in Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → Neonchord.

I can't hear anything.

Check that your iPhone isn't muted (the Ring/Silent switch or Action button), turn up the volume, and close other apps that are playing audio.

My recording is a little late or early.

Bluetooth headphones add delay. For the tightest timing, record with wired headphones or the iPhone's speaker and microphone.

Can I use the music I make?

Yes. Everything you make in Neonchord is yours, including songs started from generated ideas. All instruments are synthesized by the app; there are no third-party samples.
