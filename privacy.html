Neonchord

Privacy Policy

Effective with version 1.0 · updated October 8, 2026

Neonchord is a music-making app for iPhone and iPad. We built it to make music with, not to collect data. This policy explains what the app does with information on your device.

What we collect

We, the developer, collect nothing. Neonchord has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not send your songs, recordings or any other data to us or to anyone else.

Microphone

If you record your voice or an instrument, Neonchord asks for microphone access. Audio is recorded only while you are recording, and the recording is saved on your device as part of your song. You can turn microphone access off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

What stays on your device

Your songs, recordings, imported audio and settings are stored locally inside the app on your device. Deleting the app deletes them, so export anything you want to keep.

When you import an audio, MIDI or song file, Neonchord copies it into the app. When you export or share a song, a song file, a stem, a MIDI file, lyrics or a video, the file is created on your device and goes only where you choose to send it with the iOS share sheet.

Songs are part of your device's own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), which Apple and you control. We have no access to them.

Purchases

Neonchord Pro is an optional subscription or one-time purchase, processed entirely by Apple's App Store under Apple's Privacy Policy. The app checks whether you have Pro using Apple's StoreKit on your device. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment information, and we don't run any server that receives purchase data.

Children

Neonchord is a general-audience app. We collect no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future update ever changes what the app does with data, this policy will be updated and the effective date revised before that update ships.
